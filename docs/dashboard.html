Implemented modules

Small components, one gameplay scene and shared movement rules.

01 / HABITAT

Editable woodland

60 × 60 m environment, 20 × 20 m clearing, refuge, resting areas, obstacles and three food placeholders.

Genuine ProBuilder meshes · 1 unit = 1 metre
02 / CHARACTER

Replaceable mock blackbird

Approximately 26 cm long, with separate visuals and independent wing pivots under a stable root.

Procedural placeholder · no animation system
03 / MOVEMENT

One shared BirdMotor

Takeoff, bounded wingbeats, gliding, yaw steering, collisions, boundaries and refuge reset.

Platform-independent core · CharacterController
04 / INPUT

Separate platform adapters

Keyboard debugging, Quest joystick/trigger, two-controller motion and Android touch controls.

One active command producer per mode
05 / OBSERVATION

Reused XR infrastructure

One VRRig, tracked headset, two VR viewpoints, existing simulator and a non-VR following camera.

FoundationMode runtime switching · limitations below
06 / DELIVERY

Native build profiles

AndroidTouch and QuestStandalone select FlightPrototype with guarded scene, mode and XR policies.

Shared path: Keyboard / Quest / Touch → FlightCommand → BirdMotor + FlightDynamics → MockBlackbird. Motion detection supplies an edge through QuestFlightInput; it does not implement another motor.

Known limitations & future work

Recorded observations, not assumed root causes. No changes to flight or camera behavior in this handoff.

T020 · VR design discussion

Viewpoints and tracking origin

Physical head motion changes the viewpoint relative to the bird. Bird turns do not rotate the XR camera; the bird can leave a comfortable third-person viewing direction.

After Quest restart/re-entry, the initial view is sometimes beside the bird instead of the intended viewpoint. Investigate tracking-origin initialization, recentering and offsets.

Discuss easier switching and comfortable follow/recenter with the professor before implementation.
T020 · Diagnose before fixing

Reported lag

The student observed lag. Its cause, frame timing and latency have not been measured. Camera follow and performance must be investigated before selecting a fix.

No measured performance or VR comfort pass is claimed.
T019 · Deferred implementation

Ground locomotion

Allow forward walking and steering on the ground without requiring wingbeats. Retain the single shared motor; agree behavior and tuning in a future increment.
